Checkpoint 17 · One design, swipe, Settings

You said some screens are well done in the Flighty style and others look generic, like AI slop. I went through every screen. The generic ones are the screens built on the stock iOS form: Settings, Claude, Export, Add person, Edit contact details, Move and Deleted by retention. This checkpoint writes down the rules every screen follows, rebuilds those seven, cuts Settings from 22 rows to 9, and adds swiping. Checkpoint 16 screens already follow the rules.

Questions

Q1 · The seven stock screens are rebuilt to the rules in section 1 (frames for Settings, Add person, Export shown). Recommended: yes, all seven.
Q2 · Settings keeps only Keyboard, Documents (Summaries, Export, Claude) and Erase. The read-only rows (OCR, privacy, retention, app lock, bundle) go. Recommended: yes.
Q3 · Inside a folder, swiping sideways moves to the next folder, with the chips on top. Recents and Browse swipe too. Recommended: yes.
Q4 · Should the Vault swipe too: inside a category or a person, left or right to the next document? Recommended: no. A vault item is where you copy values, and a stray swipe there would show the wrong person’s number.
Q5 · Full-screen views swipe down to close; the scan asks first when pages are captured. Recommended: yes.
