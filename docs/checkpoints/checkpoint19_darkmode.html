Checkpoint 19 · Dark mode

The app and the keyboard follow the phone’s Light or Dark setting. Today the keyboard is pinned to light (checkpoint 15, because dark made it illegible), and most app colours are written for a white page, so dark mode is half-adapted. Every colour below has a light and a dark value, and every text colour is checked for contrast against the dark card colour (#1C1C1E). The layout does not change.

The gradient cards keep their colours; in dark mode they gain a thin light edge so a graphite card does not melt into the black page (graphite on black is only 1.5 to 1 without it).
