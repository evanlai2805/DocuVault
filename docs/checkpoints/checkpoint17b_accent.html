Checkpoint 17b · A colour that says security

You said the blue reads as a generic iOS app, not a vault. Only the blue changes; green, red, graphite, navy and clay keep their state meanings. Today the blue sits in 33 places: the tab bar, the Scan button, Unlock, Export now and other text buttons, selected chips, the star, the field labels on a vault item, the keyboard’s Open, Done and Back keys, and iOS’s own back arrows and Cancel / Save, which inherit the app tint. All three options below change every one of them through two tokens (Theme.Colors.accent and the keyboard’s Palette.accent) and the app-wide tint.

One token: accent→Filled controls: metal gradient with a top sheen→Text and icons: the solid tone, semibold→iOS tint follows
One knock-on change in every option. Search is a graphite circle today. Next to a dark metal Scan button it would read as two black buttons, so Search becomes a white glass circle with a black glass icon, exactly like the search circle in your Photos screenshot.

Gunmetal, recommended. Base #3A424C, a cool blue-grey that reads as machined steel, not black. Filled controls get a gradient that lightens through the middle with a thin top highlight, the sheen you described. It stays clearly apart from the graphite state cards, which are neutral near-black with no sheen. Text in this tone reads at 10.2 to 1 on white (today’s blue is 4.5 to 1), and white text on the metal stays at 5.8 to 1 or better.
