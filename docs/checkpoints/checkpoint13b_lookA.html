2 · What a failed check is, and how A shows it

failed check

Checkout

|

What it means. ID and card numbers carry their own check digit or letter. The NRIC’s last letter, the passport’s MRZ digits and a card’s last digit come from a formula over the other characters. If the formula disagrees, the scan almost certainly misread a character, for example 8 as B.

What A shows. A small red tag in the pill and a red ✗ on the affected key. You can still type the value; the tag tells you to check it first. When checks pass, nothing extra appears.
