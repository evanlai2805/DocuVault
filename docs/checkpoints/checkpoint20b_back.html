Checkpoint 20b · Back

Where the back button goes, now that the bar reads photo · Search · menu · vault. Each option is shown in the two places you need back: inside a folder (People, then Celine Chai) and on a document's keys (her passport). The black button is back. Swipe right on the list goes back in every option. Light mode, as on your phone now.
