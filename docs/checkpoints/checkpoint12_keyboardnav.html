Today two stacked chip rows eat 64pt and the upper one renders blurred. How password keyboards solve this: 1Password’s keyboard does not keep every item on screen — you filter by category, then pick an item, two steps. Apple’s emoji keyboard uses the other shape: a persistent category strip at the bottom edge. All three options below are drawn at 355pt.
