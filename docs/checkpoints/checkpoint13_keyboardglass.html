Checkpoint 13 · Keyboard in glass

Two things at once. First, the cut-off fix: all ten passport keys now fit at your 300pt height, so nothing scrolls and nothing clips. Second, three looks that bring the keyboard into line with the app and with iOS 26 Liquid Glass. Every keyboard is drawn at true scale, 1pt = 1px, with the iOS globe and mic band included.

iOS glass sheet→clear keyboard background→state bar · coloured like the app’s cards→glass keys · 4 rows × 44pt→glass bottom row
Why the keys clip today. At 46pt the fourth row does not fit, so the grid scrolls. iOS 26 then softens every scroll view’s edges, which is the blur you saw on Passport no. when you swiped up. Fix: keys go to 44pt. Four rows of 44 plus three 6pt gaps is 194pt, inside the 198pt left at 300pt. The scroll view, the fade and the “1 more” cue all go. The largest key set is the passport’s ten, so this holds for every document.
Why it looks off today. In your screenshot the system draws one light, rounded glass sheet. Our keyboard paints an opaque grey slab inside it. That leaves a lighter strip on top, a lighter band below, and a darker block in the middle. Every look below drops the slab and lets the system sheet show.
