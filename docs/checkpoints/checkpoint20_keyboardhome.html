Checkpoint 20 · Keyboard home

The keyboard opens on a real page: Recents, the first of four tabs: Recents, People, Documents, Favourites. Every document is on the keyboard, not only vault ones. Only vault documents need Face ID. The green circle at the right of the bar is the door to the vault. One rule runs through every screen: you always know which person and which document you are filling from. Shown in dark, as on your phone. Same 300 pt height as today; resizing and motion come in checkpoint 21. Revised after your notes: Favourites replaces Settings, the lock circle becomes the vault door.

One button, two colours. The door keeps the same texture whether open or closed: a filled gradient, a light top edge, a white glyph and a small shadow that lifts it off the bar. Only the colour changes: gunmetal when closed (the app's colour for things you tap), green when open (the app's colour for a good state). The other circles are flat glass, so the door is the one raised, filled button in the bar and reads as something to press. Both colours already exist in the app; nothing new is invented. The full-panel "Unlock with Face ID" screen goes: the keyboard always opens on a usable list.
Done is flat gunmetal now, no sheen (already changed in the code). The search pad's Open key still has its sheen.

Documents by type, then the list. With every document on the keyboard, one flat list would run to hundreds of rows. Types first matches the app's categories, so your mental map carries over. Contracts, Letters, Notes and other follow below. People stays as today: person, then their documents.

Settings live in the app only. The keyboard's Settings tab held one thing: a date format for the current session. Holding a date key already picks the format for that field and remembers it, so nothing is lost. App, Settings · Keyboard keeps the lock window and gains the start tab: Recents (default), Favourites or Last document.
Search covers everything, as now: titles, people, and for regular documents the merchant, company and party names too.

Questions

Q1 · The vault door: one raised, filled button; gunmetal with a closed lock when closed, green with an open lock when open (section 2b). Recommended: yes.
Q2 · Vault documents show with a small lock in Recents, People, Documents, Favourites and search while locked; tapping one asks for Face ID once. Recommended: yes; the alternative hides them until you open the door, which means two steps to your passport.
Q3 · Recents ordered by last touched (scanned or filled, whichever is newer), with no favourites row now that Favourites has a tab. Recommended: yes.
Q4 · Field hints fill one suggestion row and ring the matching key, and never reorder the list. Recommended: yes, sized by what the test build shows.
Q5 · Documents tab grouped by type first. Recommended: yes.
Q6 · Text lines for documents without keys: tap types the line, hold splits it into label and value. Recommended: yes.
Q8 · Open on the search pad: keep its sheen, or flat like Done? Recommended: flat like Done, so the two return keys match.
Q7 · Start tab, set in the app: Recents (default), Favourites or Last document. Recommended: yes.
