Checkpoint 18 · Chinese model: fix and first install

Two parts. First the bug: the download was never stuck. It ran, but the progress bar was cut off from it, so it sat at 0. The fix is in and checked in the Simulator: the bar now climbs to 100%. Whether the model then loads on your iPhone is still to be checked; MLX cannot run in the Simulator. Second, the install itself, designed the way Apple Intelligence and on-device AI apps introduce a big download: say what it is, how big, where it runs, check the phone is ready, then show honest progress.

Questions

Q1 · Offer it in the summary card the first time a Chinese document is summarised, once, plus the row in Settings. Recommended: yes.
Q2 · On mobile data: warn in amber and ask, with “Wait for Wi-Fi” as the other choice. Recommended: yes, ask (not block).
Q3 · Download in the background so you can leave the app, and get a notification when ready. This replaces the library’s downloader for the big file with Apple’s background download. Recommended: yes.
Q4 · After install, a “Try it” summary of a built-in sample paragraph. Recommended: yes.
Q5 · Keep the model where iOS will not clear it when storage runs low (Application Support, left out of iCloud backup), instead of Caches. Recommended: yes; you delete it yourself in Settings.
